Cooperate with The Mars Exploration Robot Motor Manufacturer Maxon to Jointly Develop High-performance Actuators for Robot Dogs
The actuator is one of the most important core components of the robot, and it is the source of the robot dog's movement ability. Therefore, WeiLan cooperate with maxon, the world's leading manufacturer in Swiss of high-precision drive systems, to jointly develop high-performance actuators for robotic dogs. The "Perseverance" probe robot and the first Mars helicopter that just arrived on Mars are equipped with maxon drive systems. Currently, more than 100 maxon motors are in service on the red Mars.
Self-awareness  Self-learning  Multi-CPU distributed computing
Not only multi-core and AI acceleration, any model of AlphaDog has at least 15 CPUs, with an industrial-grade communication system and a high-speed motion control system. Distributed computing and deep reinforcement learning technology make each AlphaDog have its own unique intelligence.
Strong Athletic Ability
In order to better support various application innovations in the real environment, in addition to the independent research and development of high-speed joint force control, dynamic balance control and model prediction-based motion control technologies similar to those of global excellent quadruped robots, WeiLan also created two original key core technologies: heuristic hybrid motion control that enables AlphaDog to adapt to various terrains at a frequency of 40,000 times per second, CV switching motion gait, mode and trajectory, and the Multi-objective enhanced motion control technology in Multi-scenes to complete various types task during motion. . What's even better is that the athletic ability of AlphaDog has been continuously improved and supports online upgrades, which is worth your expectation and you are deserved to have it.
Artificial Intelligence and Autonomous Driving Technology
AlphaDog supports powerful deep learning algorithms and mature AI technologies such as person, object, scene recognition and voice interaction. The high-end version of AlphaDog is a low-speed automatic driving device that supports advanced automatic driving technologies such as 3D map construction, automatic cruise positioning, terrain 3D structure recognition and dynamic obstacle recognition.
Aerospace Materials and Ultra-Lightweight Design
All materials used to make the AlphaDog are strictly screened, including every single screw. The core mechanical structure is made of aerospace-grade industrial aluminum and ultra-lightweight design, which ensures the mechanical strength and makes the AlphaDog move dexterously.
Carbon Fiber Materials and Automotive Industry-grade Power Transmissions
Most of the sports cars use carbon fiber materials for structural reinforcement and lightweight body, and the key lightweight components of the AlphaDog also use carbon fiber materials. At the same time, Weilan Technology teamed up with Gates, a global leader in power transmission and fluid power, to design a high-strength industrial-grade power transmission for the AlphaDog. Gates has accumulated over a hundred years and has been proven in the automotive industry, allowing AlphaDog to fully unleash its sports potential.
